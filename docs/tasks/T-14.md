## Design
- **Pixels:** the half blocks `0xDF` and `0xDC` and the full block `0xDB` give 2 vertical pixels per character cell in
  16 colours (80x50 pixels in the panel area). The shades `0xB0`-`0xB2` draw the beam; box drawing `0xB3`-`0xDA`
  draws the frames.
- **Fan:** a rotor about 9x5 cells with 4 rotation frames. The frame advances on ticks at a rate set by the
  **board-reported** duty (0 % = still; higher duty = faster; a fixed table, no floating point). Frozen and drawn in
  the halted colour when the duty is 0, the E-stop is latched or the link is lost. The duty % is printed next to it.
- **Gauge:** a half-block semicircle with marks at 30 / 90 / 150 (the kernel's servo range) and a needle drawn to the
  **reported** angle; the angle in degrees is printed under it.
- **Sensor:** a horizontal beam bar of shades from the sensor to the reading, with the 100 mm presence threshold
  marked; "PRESENCE" or "CLEAR" in words; "NO ECHO" / "STALE" in words when the data is missing or old.
- **E-stop lamp:** a block lamp with "RELEASED" / "LATCHED" in words; LINK and TELEMETRY AGE as now.
- **Never colour alone:** every state has a word. Original design: no franchise symbols, logos or fonts; no military
  wording (D-66a).
- **Layout:** keep T-08's status bar, banner, console window and decision ticker. The panel replaces the LIVE CELL rows.
  If those rows are too few, take at most 2 rows from the console window (keep at least 5 console rows). Put an
  80-column ASCII mockup of each state in `docs/evidence/T-14/README.md` before coding.

## Motion rules (T-08 rules still apply)
Nothing moves without a real value: the fan frame is driven by the reported duty; the needle and the beam jump to each
new telemetry frame (no easing); no idle animation. Rendering stays outside interrupt context where T-08 put it, and
under 1 ms per tick (measure it and report it).
